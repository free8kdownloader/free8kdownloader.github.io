Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

In short

Free 8K Downloader runs on your own computer. It has no account, collects no personal information, and never uploads your downloads, your links or your files.

What the app sends

  • Update check: the app reads a small public settings file (update.json) from this website to learn about new versions and the current price. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Download engine: on first start the app installs its open-source download components from their official publishers and keeps them up to date.
  • Pro license (paid users only): when you activate a license key, the app sends a one-way fingerprint of the key and a random, anonymous identifier of your computer to our license server, so the key can be used on a limited number of computers. The key itself, your name and your files are never sent. Free users never contact the license server.
  • Anonymous usage statistics (optional): if enabled, the app reports anonymous events such as "app opened" or "download finished" with a random install ID through Google Analytics. It includes the site name (for example "youtube"), the quality and the file format, but never the link, the file name or any personal information.
  • The sites you download from: the app connects to the site of the link you paste, just like a browser.

Payments

Payments are processed by our reseller (Lemon Squeezy). We receive your e-mail address and name to send you your license key. We never see your card details.

Your data

Settings and your license are stored only on your computer. To remove everything, uninstall the app and choose to remove its settings. To have your license record deleted from our server, contact us from the e-mail address you used to buy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
